## 10.
```
**How do I record a loop?**
Press play, then press record anywhere in the loop and play the pads. Loopstack captures exactly one full pass and closes where it started, so every take is complete and on the beat. Pressing record again won't cut a take short. Stop throws it away.
```

## 11.
```
**Can I record my voice or an instrument?**
Yes. Turn on the microphone (Mic input, or Capture sample in the Sampler) and Loopstack will ask for permission the first time. The microphone is only used while one of those features is on.
```

## 12.
```
**How does Song mode work?**
Build a loopstack, then tap Send to song. Loopstack records one full pass exactly as it sounds (every loop with its effects, mutes and solos, plus the drums) and adds it to your song as a block. Switch to Song to set repeats, reorder or duplicate blocks, and play it all back.
```

## 13.
```
**How do I get my music out of the app?**
Tap Export song on the Song screen to save the whole song as a WAV file, or use Share stems to export your loops and drums separately. Pick where it goes (Files, AirDrop, Mail or another app) from the share sheet.
```

## 14.
```
**Does it keep playing in the background?**
Yes. Your loop or song keeps playing when you leave the app or lock your phone. If the microphone is on, playback pauses instead, so the mic is never live in the background.
```

## 15.
```
**Can I play along with music from another app?**
Yes. Loopstack mixes with other apps' audio, so you can jam over a track playing somewhere else.
```

## 16.
```
**Why is the bass quiet on my phone speaker?**
Phone speakers can't play very low notes. Loopstack's FM bass is voiced to come through on a phone anyway, and headphones or speakers bring out the full low end. Turn up the FM amount for more bite.
```

## 17.
```
**What kits are included?**
Seven: Dusty, Crunch, Neon, FM, Acoustic, Studio and Hand percussion. Every kit plays all 30 grooves, and Jam mode varies them as you play.
```

## 18.
```
**Do I need an account?**
No. There's no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.
```

## 19.
```
**Where are my songs stored?**
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your songs, so export anything you want to keep.
```
