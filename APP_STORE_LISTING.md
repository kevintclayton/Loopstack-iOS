## Description
```
Loopstack is a pocket music studio with a sound of its own.

Tape that's warm, then worn out and running at the wrong speed. A deep FM bass that fills even a phone speaker. Drum kits that range from dusty, crunchy samplers to huge gated 80s snares, alongside real recorded acoustic kits. Every tone was shaped by ear to sit together, so a few loops already sound like a record.

Play in-key pads, stack loops, drop in a groove, and bend it all through tape. From slow, hazy downtempo to fast, chopped breaks, it's all in your hand.

SOUND
• Tape and Wear on the keys and the drums: warm saturation, wow and flutter, drifting pitch, dropouts
• Deep FM Bass voiced for phone speakers and big stereos alike, plus Keys, Pluck, Pad and Noise
• Shape every sound: two oscillators including FM, filter, drift, ring, glitch, release, delay and reverb
• Built-in sampler: capture your voice or any sound and play it across the pads

PLAY
• In-key pads in any key and scale: you can't hit a wrong note
• Chord mode turns one pad into a full chord (triads or 7ths)
• Arpeggiator locked to the beat: up to 4 octaves, Latch, up, down, ping-pong, as-played and random orders
• On iPhone and iPad: a two-column studio on iPad, with pads and loops side by side
• Plug in a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard: velocity, sustain pedal, pitch bend and mod wheel
• Keyboard shortcuts and musical typing on a hardware keyboard

LOOP
• Press record anywhere in the loop and it captures one full pass, closing where it started. Every take is complete.
• Stack up to 8 loops
• Record your voice into a loop with the mic, lined up with the beat
• Per loop: reverse, half speed, drive, mute, solo, volume, pan, delay and reverb

DRUMS
• Seven kits: Dusty, Crunch, Neon, FM, Acoustic, Studio and Hand percussion
• 30 grooves, from slow and dusty to fast and chopped
• Jam mode varies the groove, adds fills and moves between related grooves as you play
• Drum effects: Comp, Drive, Dirt, Vinyl, Room, Tape, Wear and Pitch

ARRANGE
• Send to Song captures your loopstack exactly as it sounds and adds it to a song as a block
• Set repeats, reorder and duplicate blocks, then play the whole song back
• Export the finished song as a WAV, or share loops and drums as stems

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• The microphone is only used when you turn it on
• Your audio stays on your device unless you choose to share it

Keep playing in the background, lock your phone, and let the loop ride.
```

## App Review Information: Notes
```
Loopstack is a music looper for iPhone and iPad. No account, sign-in or network connection is needed.

Quick test:
1. Tap "Tap to open", then tap the pads to play sounds.
2. Press the red Record button: it records one full loop pass and adds it under Layers.
3. Turn on Drums and pick a kit to add a beat.
4. Tap "Send to song", then switch to Song at the top to hear the arrangement.

Background audio: while a loop or song is playing, audio continues when the app is backgrounded or the phone is locked (the app declares the audio background mode for this). When nothing is playing, the audio engine stops.

Microphone: only used when the user turns on a mic feature (Mic input, or Capture sample in the Sampler). Audio is processed on the device and never uploaded. The mic is only heard back through headphones, never the built-in speaker.

Bluetooth: only used when the user taps Bluetooth to pair a MIDI keyboard.

MIDI: USB and Bluetooth MIDI keyboards play the instrument (optional; the app works fully without one).
```
